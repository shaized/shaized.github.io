THE ANALYST TOOLKIT

Small tools.
Faster answers.

Four everyday utilities for defenders. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you paste here is sent, stored, or logged.

TRIAGE / INDICATORS

Indicator extractor.

Paste an alert, an email, or a report. Get the unique URLs, domains, addresses, hashes, and CVE IDs, defanged and ready for a ticket. Defanged input such as hxxp and [.] is understood.

INVESTIGATION / TIMELINES

Time converter.

Turn an epoch value from a log into a readable time, or a date into epoch. Seconds, milliseconds, microseconds, and nanoseconds are detected by length. Dates without a time zone are read as UTC.

04

Enter an epoch value or a date.

What these tools do, and what they do not.

Extraction uses pattern matching. It can miss unusual formats and it treats common file endings such as .exe and .pdf as file names, not domains. Review the output before acting on it.

The CVSS calculator implements the published 3.1 base equations only; temporal and environmental metrics are not included. The KEV check compares against the dated snapshot bundled with this site, and absence from KEV is not evidence that a vulnerability is safe.
