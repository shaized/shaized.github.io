ACCESSIBILITY

Designed for more people.

Updated 5 November 2026

Our approach

The site is designed with WCAG 2.2 Level AA in mind. This statement describes the implementation and testing, not an independent certification or a claim that every legal requirement is satisfied.

Display controls

The accessibility icon on every page opens controls for text size, higher contrast, reduced motion, underlined links, and text spacing. A reset option restores the defaults. The header provides dark and light themes. These controls supplement the underlying accessible design; they do not replace it.

Keyboard and screen readers

The site uses semantic landmarks, heading hierarchies, labeled controls, visible focus, a skip link, and native dialogs. Escape closes the accessibility panel and focus returns to its opening control. Navigation, the Explore menu, news and glossary filters, pagination, playbook checklists, and fictional walkthroughs are keyboard operable. Dynamic results are announced as status updates.

Motion and media

System reduced-motion preferences take priority. The site avoids flashing content and continuously moving decorative content. The video does not autoplay and includes native playback controls. It is silent; a visible text description supplies its meaning without requiring playback. Imagery is illustrative, and meaningful images have alternatives.

Known limits

Checks cover responsive layouts, core contrast, links, and representative keyboard interactions. Comprehensive testing with assistive technology and people with disabilities has not been completed. The news search requires JavaScript; a direct source link and downloadable catalog are provided. External sites are outside this site’s control.

Report an issue

Email shaizaidenberg@gmail.com with the page, problem, and any browser or assistive technology details you are comfortable sharing. You can request the information in another format.

Animation controls

The header contains a Pause motion button. It controls decorative orbit animation, scrolling transitions, and other motion throughout the site. The homepage also offers a Pause animation control next to the investigation model. Both use the same saved preference as Reduce motion in the accessibility panel. Your system’s reduced-motion setting always takes priority.
