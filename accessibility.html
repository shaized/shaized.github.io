Built to be accessible.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

I want this portfolio to be usable by as many people as possible. Its design is guided by WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This statement describes implemented features, not an independent certification or a claim of full legal conformity.

Features

  • Semantic page landmarks, a logical heading structure, and a skip-to-content link.
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, project filters, expandable expertise sections, and project dialogs. Press Escape to close dialogs.
  • Visible keyboard focus and readable colors in dark and light themes.
  • Responsive layouts for mobile screens and browser zoom.
  • Respect for the operating system’s reduced-motion preference, plus a motion control in the footer.
  • No flashing content, autoplay audio, or endlessly running animation.
  • Text alternatives for the conceptual hero illustration; decorative diagrams are hidden from assistive technology.
  • A text-based résumé with a print layout, accessible without opening a PDF.

Known limitations

Automated checks and keyboard review cannot replace comprehensive testing with assistive technology and people with disabilities. External GitHub pages and your email application are outside this site’s control. With JavaScript disabled, the portfolio and résumé remain readable; project filtering, additional project dialogs, theme controls, and print buttons require JavaScript. Your browser’s Print command still works.

Report an accessibility issue

Please email shaizaidenberg@gmail.com with the page address, the problem, and any browser or assistive technology details you are comfortable sharing. You can also request the professional information in another format.
