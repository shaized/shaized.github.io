Cyber Fieldnotes is Shai Zaidenberg’s personal space for cybersecurity research, engineering ideas, and a general overview of SOC AI work.

News & attribution

The Cyber News section is a dated, curated edition. It is not a continuously updated feed or a real-time alert service. Each brief links to the original publisher and shows the report’s publication date. The review date indicates when the collection was checked, not when an incident occurred.

Briefs paraphrase source reporting. “Defender’s lens” and the fieldnotes are editorial interpretation and educational guidance. They are not claims that a particular organization is affected or that a suggested control is sufficient for every environment. Read the original report for scope, limitations, and subsequent updates.

Agent showcase

The showcase presents eight specialist capabilities from my SOC AI work, including agent services and analyst-invoked assistants. Descriptions explain general inputs, outputs, and boundaries. They do not publish implementation code, internal connectivity, customer data, or operational telemetry, and do not assert that every capability has the same deployment status.

Independence

Views on this site are personal. References to employers, platforms, frameworks, or publishers do not imply endorsement. No advertising, sponsorship, or affiliate links are included in this edition.
