DETECTION ENGINEERING

A quiet detection needs an explanation.

No alerts can mean no activity. It can also mean missing logs, a schema change, or a rule that no longer matches reality.

02

RESPONSIBLE AUTOMATION

Give agents a job. Give them a boundary.

An impressive answer is only part of an AI workflow. Permissions, evidence, and review paths determine whether the system is useful.

03

THREAT PRIORITIZATION

Turn “critical” into a decision.

A published severity is a starting point. Your response also depends on where the software runs, how it is exposed, and what an attacker could reach.
