CAPABILITY 02 / CONNECT THE OUTSIDE WORLD

Threat Intelligence

Connect advisories and indicators with the technologies and exposure that matter to a defender.

THE ROLE

Translate external reporting into relevant questions.

A matching technology is a lead, not proof that an organization is compromised.

Fictional demonstration

All events, systems, identities, and outputs below are invented for explanation. Nothing runs against real infrastructure.

INTERACTIVE WALKTHROUGHILLUSTRATIVE ONLY

SAMPLE INPUT

A fictional advisory for Northstar Gateway 4.2 and a sample asset inventory.

STEP 1 OF 4

Read the report

The invented advisory describes an authentication bypass in Northstar Gateway 4.2. It has no real CVE identifier.

STEP 2 OF 4

Check relevance

Sample inventory contains one internet-facing gateway on version 4.2 and two internal gateways on 4.4.

STEP 3 OF 4

Separate facts from gaps

The version match and exposure are demonstrated in the sample. Exploitation and effective mitigations have not been verified.

STEP 4 OF 4

Analyst next step

Validate the inventory, obtain vendor guidance, and assign the exposed asset to its owner for review.

WHAT A USEFUL OUTPUT LOOKS LIKE

Evidence. Uncertainty.
A clear next step.

The aim is to make an analyst’s decision better informed. Keep supporting evidence visible, separate facts from assumptions, and explain what still needs checking.
