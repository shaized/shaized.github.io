PRIVACY

A small digital footprint.

Updated 5 November 2026

What the site does

This is Shai Zaidenberg’s personal cybersecurity website. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, embedded social widgets, or contact forms. Its own code does not set cookies. Images, video, fonts, styles, and scripts are served with the site.

Your display preferences

Theme, text size, contrast, spacing, underlining, and motion choices are stored locally in this browser. They are not sent to me. You can reset them in the accessibility panel or remove them in your browser’s site-data settings.

The CISA news feed

The news page requests a public JSON catalog from raw.githubusercontent.com, CISA’s official GitHub data mirror, when you open it or choose Refresh. GitHub receives the connection information required to serve that request, including your IP address. The request sends no login credentials and no referrer. Search and filter values stay in the page and are not transmitted. If the request fails, the page uses a dated bundled snapshot. There is no automatic background polling.

Learning tools

Playbook checkmarks and glossary searches remain in the current page. They are not saved to storage or sent to a server. Navigating away or reloading resets the playbook checkmarks.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages hosts this site and receives connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure it. GitHub controls its infrastructure logs and retention. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. LinkedIn, GitHub, email applications, and linked publishers have their own privacy practices; no social widgets load here.

Contact and requests

If you email me, I receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use it to respond. Do not send credentials or confidential incident data. For privacy questions or requests about messages you have sent, contact shaizaidenberg@gmail.com.
