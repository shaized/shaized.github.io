Privacy, by design.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This is the personal portfolio of Shai Zaidenberg. It presents professional experience and provides links to get in touch.

What this website collects

This site does not run analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, embedded social widgets, or contact forms. The site’s own code does not set cookies or send visitor information to an application server. All fonts, styles, scripts, and artwork are served with the site.

Your display preferences

If you change the color theme or motion preference, the site stores that choice in your browser’s local storage under sz-theme and sz-motion. These values stay on your device, are not sent to me, and can be removed through your browser’s site-data settings. Your system’s reduced-motion setting takes priority.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives connection information, including IP addresses, as part of serving and securing the website. GitHub’s processing is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This website does not control GitHub’s infrastructure logs or retention.

Email & external links

Email links open your email application. If you send me a message, I receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use it to respond to your inquiry. Please do not send credentials or confidential incident data. External links take you to services with their own privacy practices.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent to me, email shaizaidenberg@gmail.com.
