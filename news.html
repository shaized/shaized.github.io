Know what this feed tells you.

KEV is a catalog of known exploitation, not a complete cyber news wire or a real-time incident feed. “Added” means added to CISA’s catalog, not when exploitation began. “Unknown” ransomware use does not mean the vulnerability is safe.

CISA-listed due dates are shown as source data, not universal remediation deadlines. Determine applicable obligations and urgency for your own environment. This independent site is not affiliated with CISA.
