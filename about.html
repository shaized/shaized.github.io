BEHIND THE WORK

Shai Zaidenberg.
Engineer. Defender. Builder.

I work where security operations, cloud infrastructure, and AI meet.

SECURITY / SYSTEMS / HUMAN JUDGMENT

MY PERSPECTIVE

Understand the system.
Follow the evidence.

My background spans NOC operations, CloudOps, security engineering, and incident response. Working across those layers shaped how I approach problems: start with what the system is actually doing, understand the gaps, and make the next action clear.

I build AI-assisted capabilities for defenders and explore how automation can make investigation, intelligence, and documentation more useful. The aim is thoughtful support for the people making consequential decisions.

This site is a public space for that work: general capability overviews, practical fieldnotes, and a window into the wider threat landscape.

HOW I APPROACH THE WORK

Principles before tools.

01

Evidence first.

Make conclusions traceable. Keep observations separate from assumptions.

02

Systems thinking.

Follow the path across identity, endpoint, cloud, data, and the people operating them.

03

Human judgment.

Use automation to strengthen decisions. Make its permissions and limits explicit.
